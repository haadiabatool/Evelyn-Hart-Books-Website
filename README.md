# 📚 Evelyn Hart – Online Book Store

Evelyn Hart is a modern and responsive online bookstore designed to provide readers with a smooth and engaging book-shopping experience. The website features a curated collection of books focused on personal growth, motivation, inspiration, mindfulness, and life lessons.

Users can explore books, search by title or keyword, filter products by category and price range, add books to their wishlist, and manage their shopping cart through an interactive interface.

## ✨ Features

##🏠 Hero Section

Attractive landing page with an engaging introduction.

Clear call-to-action buttons to explore the book collection.

## 📖 Book Collection

Display a collection of books with cover images, titles, prices, and details.

Organized book cards for easy browsing.

## 🔍 Search Functionality

Search books by title or keyword.

Quickly find specific books from the collection.

## 🗂️ Category Filtering

Filter books according to different categories:

All

Personal Growth

Motivation

Inspiration

Mindfulness

Life Lessons

## 💰 Price Filtering

Filter books according to different price ranges.

Find books that match your budget.

## ❤️ Wishlist

Add favorite books to the wishlist.

Manage saved books for future purchases.

## 🛒 Shopping Cart

Add books to the shopping cart.

Manage selected products.

View cart items and their prices.

## 🧾 Cart Total

Automatically calculate the total price of selected books.

Update the total when cart items change.
